# Orange4Home trial: Section 6 models on a non-CASAS house (preliminary)

Generated by `python -m tist_revision.pipeline.orange_trial report`; do not edit. Findings are logged in `tist_revision/EXPLORATIONS.md`. Not part of the paper.

One occupant, 20 work days, on/off and open/closed sources only (presence, doors, switches, lights, shutters, TV status), state changes only; labels are the 20 classes of the dataset paper.

Protocol as in Section 6: clusters and supervised models are fit on continuous `FL_20` train windows (20 events, 50% overlap), tested on pre-segmented `FL_20_p` windows of held-out days (20% of the days); L1 labels (resident id removed), macro F1 over classes with ≥ 50 pool and ≥ 20 test windows. DISCOVER = MLM encoder + time-of-day histogram + SCAN (balanced); ≈100 = K 20 × 5 labels, ≈250 = K 50 × 5, all = K 50 with every pool window labeled. Mean ± std over seeds.

"Twin": another test window with exactly the same events and a different label (copies of one event in two resident streams). No method can label both twins correctly; the ceiling is the best achievable accuracy.
