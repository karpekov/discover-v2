# Kyoto7 (twor.2009) trial: Section 6 models under several ways of handling the two residents

Generated by `python -m tist_revision.pipeline.kyoto_trial report`; do not edit. Findings are logged in `tist_revision/EXPLORATIONS.md`. Not part of the paper.

Protocol as in Section 6: clusters and supervised models are fit on continuous `FL_20` train windows (20 events, 50% overlap), tested on pre-segmented `FL_20_p` windows of held-out days (20% of the days); L1 labels (resident id removed), macro F1 over classes with ≥ 50 pool and ≥ 20 test windows. DISCOVER = MLM encoder + time-of-day histogram + SCAN (balanced); ≈100 = K 20 × 5 labels, ≈250 = K 50 × 5, all = K 50 with every pool window labeled. Mean ± std over seeds.

## Variants

| Variant | Dataset | Sensor stream | Label of an event |
|---|---|---|---|
| Merged, sensor-attributed labels | `kyotoaff` | all events, once, in time order (windows of `kyotomerged`) | of the running activities, the one that most often fires the event's sensor when it runs alone |
| Merged, household-state labels | `kyotojoint` | all events, once, in time order (windows of `kyotomerged`) | household state: all running activities, e.g. `Sleep+Work` |
| Resident streams, no copies | `kyotoattr` | one stream per resident and one for activities without a resident id; each event goes into one stream only, that of its sensor-attributed activity | the sensor-attributed activity |
